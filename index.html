Good to know

A few quick answers.

Explore the sample shop to see how a day’s work can fit together.

What is ShelfWise?

A shop management workspace for sales, billing, inventory, purchases, customer credit and business insights.

Who can use it?

Small shop owners and local vendors who want a clearer view of everyday business records.

How does QR scanning work?

Products can have unique QR identifiers. Scan a code to add an item to a bill, then confirm payment to record the sale.

Does inventory update automatically?

In the demo, stock changes only after a sale or purchase is confirmed.

Can I track my profit?

The demo estimates gross and net profit from recorded sales, product costs and expenses.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes, the web experience adapts to mobile browsers. A separate native app is not part of this prototype.

Is a demo available?

Yes. Explore Demo opens the sample shop without registration.
